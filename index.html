THE MISSION

Equitable access to music education for every student.

Since 2012, We've Got Rhythm has worked to reduce socioeconomic barriers to music and the performing arts. Through our BEATS (Bringing Equipment & Access To Students) program and #RhythmSupport concert and event series, we provide young people with free instruments, live performance experiences, transportation when needed, and meaningful access to artists and cultural institutions.

We believe a world-class performance should never feel like a distant privilege. And we know that sometimes a free ticket is not enough.

That is why our support can extend beyond admission. We provide tickets for parents and guardians so that a concert can become a shared family experience. We help provide transportation for school and community groups when getting to the venue would otherwise be a barrier. And whenever possible, we bring young people and their families backstage at renowned venues such as Carnegie Hall, giving them opportunities to meet and learn from the artists who inspire them.

For us, access means more than getting through the door. It means helping young people feel that these experiences, these spaces, and the possibilities they represent belong to them too.
