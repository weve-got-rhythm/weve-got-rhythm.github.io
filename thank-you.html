YOUR SUPPORT MATTERS

Thank You for Believing in Our Young Musicians

Your generosity helps We’ve Got Rhythm open the doors of Carnegie Hall to young people and expand equitable access to music education across our communities.

"Experience inspires young musicians to achieve and reimagine their own potential."

We've Got Rhythm · Est. 2012

Your contribution directly funds the logistical and educational scaffolding, including charter group transportation, school liaisons, and family access, that makes this experience possible for approximately 75 students this December 27.

Beyond this performance, your partnership strengthens our ongoing work placing musical instruments directly into students' hands through our BEATS program.

*We’ve Got Rhythm is a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt nonprofit organization. An official written acknowledgment for your records will follow from our organization. If you gave through PayPal, PayPal also provides an initial email receipt.*
